ipe() (“we”) respects your privacy and follows applicable law, including Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information our websites and mobile apps handle and how.

**The short version**: services covered by this policy do not ask for accounts, names or phone numbers. Our apps mostly keep data on your device, and the apps covered today use no advertising SDK; exceptions are in Appendix B. We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

1. **Google Analytics 4** sets cookies (`_ga`, up to two years) to count visits. We do not put directly identifying information such as names or emails into analytics. Google may nevertheless process cookie identifiers and IP-based data. Measurement ID `[confirm: whether to show it publicly]`.
2. **Cloudflare Web Analytics** measures in aggregate without cookies (home, idle, still, draft — Appendix A).
3. **Google AdSense** is used only on sites that serve ads, and may be served there (Appendix A — no ad code found in current source; actual serving `[confirm]`). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to our site or other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or opt out at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info) (Europe: [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu)). How Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Before ads appear on any new site we update this policy and Appendix A first.
4. **Consent and choice**: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent (Google-certified CMP with Consent Mode) before analytics or ad cookies are stored. Korean visitors are currently not shown a consent banner. `[confirm: whether to show a consent banner to Korean visitors]` You can block or delete cookies in your browser or use Google’s [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout); the site works the same without them.
5. **Apps**: the apps covered by this policy currently use no advertising SDK (Appendix B, pubspec checked 2026-11-11). If this changes we update this policy and the store disclosures first.
6. **Google Fonts and other external resources**: some pages may send your IP address to Google when loading fonts. `[confirm: which sites self-host fonts — tidy Appendix A]`

## 7. Deletion

When the purpose is fulfilled or retention ends, we delete data without delay, by methods that prevent recovery. Data stored only on your device disappears when you delete the app.

## 8. Your rights

You may at any time request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing, and withdraw consent.
- **How to ask**: write to the contact in section 1 (privacy@ipe.rest); we tell you the outcome within 10 days.
- **Representatives**: a legal guardian or an authorised representative may make the request, and we may ask for proof of the authority (e.g. a power of attorney) `[confirm: how we verify]`.
- **If we decline**: we tell you the reason and how to object. Analytics (GA4) data is not tied to your name or email, so in some cases we cannot locate an individual’s records; in that case it may be impractical to act, and we may decline and tell you why `[confirm: legal check]`. Email correspondence we can locate and delete.
- **Objection**: if you disagree with the outcome you may ask again at the same address and apply for relief to the bodies in section 11.
- If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you also have GDPR rights (objection, portability, complaint to a supervisory authority).

## 9. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 14 in Korea (or under 13 in the US under COPPA, under the age set by your country within 13–16 in the EU under GDPR, or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information or knowingly show them personalised ads. If we learn we have, we delete it promptly. `[confirm: App Store age rating; whether we apply for Google Play “Designed for Families” (which adds Families-policy limits on ads and analytics) — do not apply before deciding]`
