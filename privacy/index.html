Privacy Policy — still()

Effective date: 2026-11-01

The short version: the App collects no personal data. The Website (still.ipe.rest) is separate — it measures visits with Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics, as described in section 2. Neither one asks for your name or email.

still() ("the App") is a Bible-companion app operated by Davy Kim (ipe Studio), an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy also covers our website still.ipe.rest ("the Website"). The App and the Website are two different things: section 1 is about the App only, and section 2 is about the Website only.

1. The App — information we collect: none

  • The App: what you type, how you feel, saved verses, and notes are processed and stored only on your device, encrypted locally. They are never transmitted to us or any server, never stored on our systems, and never seen by us. The App contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Sensitive information: because still() is a faith app, your entries may relate to religious belief. Such content is processed on-device only and is never collected, transmitted, or stored by us.
  • Nothing in this section changes when you visit the Website, and nothing the Website measures comes from the App.

2. The Website (still.ipe.rest) — visit analytics

This section applies only to the Website in your browser — not to the App.

  • Google Analytics 4: we use it to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as _ga, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside your country.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: a privacy-first service that sets no cookies and does not track or profile individual visitors. It gives us basic page-view counts.
  • Stored in your browser: the language you choose is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device and is never sent to us.
  • Email you send us: if you write to ipe.haven@gmail.com, we receive your address and what you write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that.
  • Hosting: the Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives technical request data such as your IP address under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Opting out: block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The Website works the same without them. We use this data to understand what is read and to improve the site — not for profiling, and we never sell it.

3. On-device processing (App)

still() uses an on-device AI model to match a comforting, human-reviewed Bible verse to your words, locally on your device. The App downloads the model file from our servers — a one-way download; nothing you enter is ever uploaded.

4. Subscriptions & purchases (App)

Optional subscriptions (e.g., "Still & Deep") are processed by Apple via the App Store. Apple handles payment and receives the transaction; we never receive your payment details, card number, or Apple ID. Your subscription status is verified on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third parties

  • Apple — App distribution and subscription processing (the App).
  • Google (Google Analytics 4) and Cloudflare (Web Analytics) — visit statistics for the Website only, as described in section 2.
  • GitHub — hosting of the Website.

The App sends nothing to Google, Cloudflare or GitHub. We use no advertising networks or data brokers, and we never sell data. We share information with no one else except where the law requires.

6. Security

Content you create in the App is stored encrypted on your device and never transmitted off it. We practice strict data minimization: the App collects nothing, and the Website collects only the visit statistics described in section 2.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of any personal information. For the App we hold none, so there is nothing on our side to act on; content on your device is removed when you delete the App or clear its data. Website analytics data is not tied to your name or email, so we usually cannot find an individual in it; email correspondence we can locate and delete. For any request, contact ipe.haven@gmail.com.

8. Children

still() is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your region). The App collects no personal information from anyone, and the Website asks for none.

9. Privacy Officer

Responsible for personal-information handling and complaints.

still() offers comfort and Scripture, not counseling, medical, or emergency services. In a crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line shown in the App.

For the Website, Google Analytics processes visit statistics (including cookie identifiers) on our behalf; you can block this as described in section 2. You retain all statutory rights; contact us to exercise any of them.

still()("본 앱")은 개인 개발자 Davy Kim (ipe Studio)("당사")이 운영하는 성경 동반 앱입니다. 본 방침은 웹사이트 still.ipe.rest("본 사이트")에도 적용됩니다. 앱과 웹사이트는 서로 다릅니다. 1항은 앱에만, 2항은 웹사이트에만 해당합니다.

1. 앱 — 수집하는 개인정보: 없음

  • 앱: 입력한 내용, 감정, 저장한 말씀, 메모는 기기 내에서만 처리되고 암호화 저장되며, 당사나 어떤 서버로도 전송되지 않고 저장·열람되지 않습니다. 앱에는 분석·추적 SDK가 없습니다.
  • 민감정보: 신앙 앱 특성상 종교적 신념과 관련된 내용이 있을 수 있으나, 전부 기기 내에서만 처리되며 당사가 수집·전송·저장하지 않습니다.
  • 웹사이트를 방문해도 이 항의 내용은 달라지지 않으며, 웹사이트가 측정하는 것은 앱에서 오지 않습니다.

2. 웹사이트(still.ipe.rest) — 방문 분석

이 항은 브라우저로 보는 웹사이트에만 적용되며, 앱에는 적용되지 않습니다.

  • Google Analytics 4: 어떤 페이지가 읽히는지 보기 위해 사용합니다. 페이지 조회, 유입 경로, 브라우저·기기 종류, 대략적인 위치(국가 또는 도시), 사이트 밖으로 나가는 링크 클릭(링크 주소만)을 기록합니다. 방문을 구분하기 위해 브라우저에 쿠키(예: _ga, 최대 2년)를 저장합니다. 분석에서 이름이나 이메일을 요구하지 않으며, 이로써 개인을 식별할 수 없습니다. 데이터는 Google이 당사를 대신해 처리하며, 국외 서버가 포함될 수 있습니다.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: 쿠키를 설정하지 않고 개별 방문자를 추적·프로파일링하지 않는 서비스로, 기본적인 페이지 조회 수를 제공합니다.
  • 브라우저에 저장되는 것: 선택한 언어는 이용자 기기의 브라우저 로컬 저장소에만 보관되며 당사로 전송되지 않습니다.
  • 보내 주신 이메일: ipe.haven@gmail.com 으로 연락하시면 이메일 주소와 쓰신 내용을 받게 됩니다. 답변 목적으로만 쓰고, 그에 필요한 기간 동안만 보관합니다.
  • 호스팅: 웹사이트는 GitHub Pages에서 제공되며, IP 주소 등 접속에 필요한 기술 정보는 GitHub의 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 GitHub가 받습니다.
  • 거부 방법: 브라우저 설정에서 쿠키를 차단·삭제하거나 Google의 Analytics 차단 부가기능을 사용하세요. 쿠키가 없어도 웹사이트는 똑같이 동작합니다. 이 데이터는 무엇이 읽히는지 파악하고 사이트를 개선하는 데에만 쓰며, 프로파일링하지 않고 판매하지 않습니다.
