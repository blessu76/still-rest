Privacy Policy — still()

Effective date: (to be published)

The short version — and the whole version: we collect no personal data. Not through the App, and not through the Website.

still() ("the App") is a Bible-companion app operated by Davy Kim (ipe Studio), an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy also covers our website still.ipe.rest ("the Website").

1. Information we collect — none

  • The App: what you type, how you feel, saved verses, and notes are processed and stored only on your device, encrypted locally. They are never transmitted to us or any server, never stored on our systems, and never seen by us. The App contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The Website: uses Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-first, cookieless service that sets no cookies, uses no fingerprinting, and does not track or profile individual visitors.
  • Sensitive information: because still() is a faith app, your entries may relate to religious belief. Such content is processed on-device only and is never collected, transmitted, or stored by us.

2. On-device processing

still() uses an on-device AI model to match a comforting, human-reviewed Bible verse to your words, locally on your device. The App downloads the model file from our servers — a one-way download; nothing you enter is ever uploaded.

3. Subscriptions & purchases

Optional subscriptions (e.g., "Still & Deep") are processed by Apple via the App Store. Apple handles payment and receives the transaction; we never receive your payment details, card number, or Apple ID. Your subscription status is verified on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

  • Apple — App distribution and subscription processing.

Besides Apple, our only third-party service is Cloudflare Web Analytics (privacy-first, cookieless — no personal data collected). We use no advertising networks or data brokers, and we never sell data.

5. Security

Content you create is stored encrypted on your device and never transmitted off it. We practice strict data minimization: we collect nothing.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of any personal information. Because we hold none, there is nothing on our side to act on; content on your device is removed when you delete the App or clear its data. For any request, contact ipe.haven@gmail.com.

7. Children

still() is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your region), and collects no personal information from anyone regardless.

8. Privacy Officer

  • Name: Davy Kim (ipe Studio) · Contact: ipe.haven@gmail.com

Responsible for personal-information handling and complaints.

10. Not medical or crisis care

still() offers comfort and Scripture, not counseling, medical, or emergency services. In a crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line shown in the App.

11. International users (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we collect no personal data, there is no processing to access, delete, object to, or transfer. You retain all statutory rights; contact us to exercise any of them.
